## 中文

### 0.6.9 · 菜单栏打开体验修复

- 修复刚启动时左键点击菜单栏图标无法展开的问题。额度与本机用量尚在读取时，也能先打开面板。
- 修复快速点击后面板闪一下就消失的问题：同一次点击的延迟系统回调不会再重复触发开关。
- 保留再次点击关闭、点击外部收起、Esc 关闭及面板内子窗口的交互行为。
- 增加启动、快速点击、重复回调、焦点切换及空数据状态的回归检查。

账户额度、本机 Token、参考费用、服务检测、中英文切换与跟随系统的外观功能继续保留。

### 安装与升级

下载 `NowBar-0.6.9-macOS-arm64.zip`，解压后退出旧版，用新的 `NowBar.app` 替换「应用程序」中的旧应用，再重新打开。已有设置与本机历史保留。

适用于 **Apple Silicon（M 系列芯片）与 macOS 14 或更新版本**。版本号 **0.6.9（build 21）**。安装包使用本机临时签名，尚未经过 Apple 公证；附 SHA-256 校验文件。

应用会自动检查并提醒新版；下载后仍需手动替换安装。

## English

### 0.6.9 · Menu bar interaction fixes

- Fixed left clicks failing to open the menu bar panel immediately after launch. The panel can open while quota and local usage data are still loading.
- Fixed the panel briefly appearing and immediately disappearing after a quick click. A delayed system callback for the same click no longer toggles the panel twice.
- Preserved click-to-close, outside-click dismissal, Escape, and interactions with windows attached to the panel.
- Added regression checks for startup, quick clicks, duplicate callbacks, focus changes, and empty-data states.

Account quotas, local token usage, estimated costs, service detection, Chinese/English localization, and system-following appearance remain available.

### Install or upgrade

Download and extract `NowBar-0.6.9-macOS-arm64.zip`. Quit NowBar, replace the existing app in Applications with the new `NowBar.app`, and reopen it. Saved preferences and local history are preserved.

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later**. Version **0.6.9 (build 21)**. The app is locally ad hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized. A separate SHA-256 checksum is included.

NowBar automatically checks for new releases and displays a notification. Downloaded updates still require replacing the app manually.
