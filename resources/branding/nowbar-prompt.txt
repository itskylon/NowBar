Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production macOS app icon for NowBar, a refined AI usage and quota menu-bar utility.
Primary request: Design a very simple, premium, distinctive geometric N monogram whose two upright strokes feel like usage bars. Single app icon only, front-on, centered, square canvas, no presentation board.
Design: one immaculate near-black graphite rounded-square macOS tile occupying 86 percent of the canvas width, with generous uniform transparent margin outside the tile. The monogram occupies about 52 percent of the tile width and 54 percent of its height. All strokes optically balanced, thick, clean, softly eased corners; ample negative space. The mark should remain recognizable at 16 and 32 pixels.
Style: extremely restrained flat vector-like identity, understated precision, calm professional Mac utility. Solid deep graphite tile, solid off-white mark, single muted mint accent. Sharp geometry and smooth edges.
Constraints: genuine alpha outside the rounded tile. Tile and monogram opaque. No words, no typography except the N-shaped symbol, no letters spelling NowBar, no mockups, no extra icons, no borders, no decorative circuitry, no bars chart next to the monogram, no sparkles, no gradients, no bevel, no texture, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no tiny details, no watermark.
